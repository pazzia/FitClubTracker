# FitClub 0.6.2 visual changes

- Exercise photos use their decoded aspect ratio in the swipe carousel. Continuous white edge padding is trimmed; interior white objects are preserved.
- Catalog and picker thumbnails are 104×82 dp, use a cropped fill and sampled bitmaps. Foreground contrast is increased. A bundled ML Kit SINGLE_IMAGE_MODE person mask fades distant background; an expanded envelope around the athlete preserves nearby equipment. Segmentation has a two-second timeout and an image-only fallback. No photo is sent to a server. The model is bundled in the APK.
- The male glute atlas image is unchanged. A separate narrow dark posing-brief layer is drawn in source coordinates: waistband above the highlighted muscles and a thin central strap. The woman's glute image is unchanged.
- Exercise maps use the user's base athlete appearance, with natural flesh tones rather than the old gray mannequins. Each figure has aligned independent primary/secondary masks; uninvolved regions retain the natural skin colour. Existing sports clothing remains opaque. Glute and covered chest regions are marked over the corresponding garment surface.
